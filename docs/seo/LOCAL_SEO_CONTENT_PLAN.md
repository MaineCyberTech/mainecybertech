# Local SEO Content Plan — Maine Cyber Tech

## Positioning

Maine Cyber Tech provides managed IT services, cybersecurity support, Microsoft 365 administration, business Wi-Fi, network installation, UniFi systems, security cameras, cloud backup, disaster recovery planning, and technology consulting for Maine organizations.

## Primary local content clusters

### 1. Managed IT / MSP Support

Target people searching for practical IT support, outsourced IT, small business technology support, or help replacing informal break/fix support.

Example topics:

- Small Business IT Support Checklist for Maine Companies
- What Does a Managed IT Provider Do for a Small Business?
- Break/Fix IT vs Managed IT: Which Is Better for Maine Small Businesses?
- 15 Signs Your Business Has Outgrown DIY IT Support

### 2. Cybersecurity / Microsoft 365 Security

Target business owners and campground administrators looking for plain-English security guidance.

Example topics:

- Microsoft 365 Security Checklist for Maine Small Businesses
- Cybersecurity Checklist for Maine campgrounds and Small Businesses
- Why Every Business Should Enable MFA Before Anything Else
- What to Do After a Suspicious Login Alert in Microsoft 365

### 3. Wi-Fi / Networking / UniFi

Target local facility managers, restaurants, marinas, warehouses, and offices with coverage, cabling, or outdoor Wi-Fi needs.

Example topics:

- Business Wi-Fi Planning Checklist for Maine Buildings
- How to Improve Wi-Fi in restaurants, Marinas, Warehouses, and Older Buildings
- UniFi Network Setup for Small Businesses: What to Plan Before Installation
- Outdoor Wi-Fi Planning Guide for Maine Properties

### 4. Security Cameras / Physical Security

Target local organizations considering security cameras, NVRs, UniFi Protect, PoE cameras, and remote access.

Example topics:

- Security Camera System Planning Checklist for Maine Businesses
- UniFi Protect vs Traditional Camera Systems for Small Businesses
- How Many Security Cameras Does a Small Business Need?
- NVR, PoE Cameras, Cabling, and Remote Access: Plain-English Guide

### 5. Cloud Backup / Disaster Recovery

Target business owners trying to understand backup, restore tests, continuity, and cloud planning.

Example topics:

- Cloud Backup Checklist for Maine Small Businesses
- Backup vs Disaster Recovery: What Small Businesses Need to Know
- What Files Should a Small Business Back Up?
- How to Create a Simple Business Continuity Plan

### 6. Vertical-Specific Local SEO

Target niches aligned with Maine Cyber Tech's proposal/project work.

Example topics:

- IT Support for restaurants and campgrounds in Maine: What to Review First
- Technology Checklist for Marinas, Warehouses, and Outdoor Facilities
- Phone, Internet, Wi-Fi, and Security Camera Planning for restaurants
- Network and Camera Planning for Boat Storage and Warehouse Buildings

## Internal linking rules

- Every blog post links to `/contact`.
- Every blog post links to at least one relevant service page.
- Service pages link to related blog posts.
- Homepage links to all major service pages.
- Blog category/listing page links to priority service pages.

## Content quality rules

- Helpful, practical, people-first content.
- No keyword stuffing.
- No exaggerated claims.
- No unsupported compliance/security guarantees.
- Local context should be natural, not forced.
- Write for business owners and administrators, not just engineers.
