# Business Wi-Fi Planning Checklist for Maine Buildings

Reliable Wi-Fi depends on more than buying a few access points. Building materials, cable paths, outdoor areas, user density, equipment placement, power, and internet service all affect the final result.

This checklist is designed for Maine offices, churches, marinas, warehouses, and local facilities planning a Wi-Fi upgrade.

## Map the coverage areas

- Offices
- Meeting rooms
- Warehouses
- Outdoor areas
- Parking lots
- Gates or signs
- Detached buildings
- Public/guest areas

## Review cabling and conduit

- Are cable paths available?
- Is conduit usable?
- Is there a pull string?
- Are there distance limitations?
- Is outdoor-rated cable required?

## Separate business and guest traffic

Guest Wi-Fi should usually be separated from internal business systems. This helps protect staff devices, business applications, and network equipment.

## Plan access point placement

Access points should be placed based on coverage needs, building shape, mounting options, and cable routes. Outdoor Wi-Fi often requires weather-rated equipment and careful placement.
