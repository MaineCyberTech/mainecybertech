# MCT Web Store V5 - Analytics, Automation, Proposal, and Sales Ops Implementation Guide

Generated: 2026-07-29

This pack adds the operational layer that turns the Maine Cyber Tech web store into a lead, quote, proposal, project, SEO, and fulfillment engine.

## Features

- **Store Analytics + Conversion Tracking**: Track views, CTA clicks, quiz events, quote events, promo interactions, and conversion paths.
- **Lead Scoring**: Score quote requests and service finder results based on urgency, selected products, business size, and buying intent.
- **Recommendation Engine V2**: Advanced recommendation groups such as frequently paired, next step, upgrade path, monthly match, dependency, and seasonal match.
- **Product Comparison Pages**: Generate structured comparison pages for common product and package decisions.
- **Good Better Best Package Ladders**: Show guided three-step choices per category.
- **Quote-to-Proposal Generator**: Generate internal quote review, client-facing proposal draft, scope, assumptions, exclusions, add-ons, and follow-up email.
- **Intake-to-Project Conversion**: Convert quote/intake submissions into organization/contact/project/ticket/task scaffolds later.
- **Admin Product Lifecycle Workflow**: Draft, review, published, seasonal, paused, archived, needs update, and compliance review lifecycle.
- **Store Content Quality Auditor**: Audit product copy for clarity, completeness, risk, unsupported claims, and SEO quality.
- **SEO Landing Page Generator**: Generate local and vertical service landing page scaffolds from catalog data.
- **FAQ and Objection Handling System**: Reusable product/category FAQs with FAQ schema readiness.
- **Review/Testimonial Placement System**: Admin-approved testimonials with permission and provenance tracking.
- **Case Study Generator**: Turn completed work into publishable case study drafts with approval controls.
- **Email Nurture Sequences**: Follow-up content for leads by interest area and funnel stage.
- **Customer Portal Service Hub**: Expose requested services, quote status, proposals, projects, docs, tasks, and recommended next services in portal.
- **Internal Fulfillment Checklist Generator**: Generate task checklists from product/service definitions.
- **Product Profitability + Effort Scoring**: Score products by effort, complexity, margin, lead value, onsite needs, admin access, risk, seasonality.
- **Product Dependency Engine**: Define required/recommended prerequisites and quote/admin warnings.
- **Public Service Readiness Checklist Downloads**: Lead magnet checklists for cyber insurance, starter IT, website, onboarding/offboarding, marinas, churches, backups.
