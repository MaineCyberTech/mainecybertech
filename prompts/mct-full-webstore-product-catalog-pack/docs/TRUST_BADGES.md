# Trust Badges

## Badges included

- No-secret intake.
- Plain-English report.
- Local Maine support.
- Scoped deliverables.
- Upgrade path available.
- Consult required for sensitive work.

## Placement

- Product card: max 2 badges.
- Product detail: max 4 badges.
- Intake form: always show No-secret intake.
- Sensitive work: always show Consult required for sensitive work.

## Purpose

These badges build trust without making unsupported claims. They clarify how Maine Cyber Tech works and reduce buying anxiety.
