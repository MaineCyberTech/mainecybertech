# Maine Cyber Tech Full Internal Fulfillment Procedures

## Global Guardrails

- Never request, accept, or store passwords, MFA recovery codes, API keys, private keys, or other secrets in public forms or normal notes.
- Use least privilege and temporary/delegated access wherever possible.
- Document approval before tenant, DNS, backup restore, offboarding, firewall, VLAN, or camera remote-access changes.
- Separate public marketing content from internal operations content.
- Store only necessary evidence and redact sensitive data.
- Provide a plain-English customer closeout for every item.
